── Useful Commands ─────────────────────────────────

Check pods:

  kubectl get pods -l app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }}

Check services:

  kubectl get svc

View logs:

  kubectl logs -l app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }}

Upgrade release:

  helm upgrade {{ .Release.Name }} .

Rollback release:

  helm rollback {{ .Release.Name }} <REVISION>

Uninstall release:

  helm uninstall {{ .Release.Name }}
